Nicotine+ Sync

Follow SoundCloud channels and playlists. When a track shows up, Nicotine+ Sync looks for it on Soulseek and downloads the best copy it can find.

  1. Before you start

    Nicotine+ Sync searches Soulseek, a network where people share their own music folders. You decide what you download and share. Only download music you have the right to have, such as free downloads, promos sent to you, or tracks you've bought. You're responsible for how you use it.

  2. Connect to Soulseek

    Nicotine+ Sync adds a small plugin to Nicotine+ so the two apps can talk. It only works on this computer.

    Use a separate account just for Nicotine+ Sync. A name that doesn't exist yet is created automatically when you log in. Logging in with the same account in another Soulseek app will disconnect one of them.

  3. Where tracks go

    Many people on Soulseek only let you download if you share something too. Sharing means others can download these files from you.
